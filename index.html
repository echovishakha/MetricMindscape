🔍 KPI Audit Tool

Identify redundant metrics and focus on what really matters for business outcomes

One sales org optimized for "calls made per day" where reps simply called voicemails to hit numbers. A design team hit 100% "feedback resolution rate" by auto-responding "thanks!" Your dashboard tracks 27 KPIs, but only two actually matter. The rest? Noise.

The KPI Audit Tool is an AI-powered application that helps you identify which metrics genuinely drive business outcomes and which ones might be creating unnecessary noise or misleading your team.

✅ Impact Score Analysis

The tool calculates an impact score for each metric based on multiple factors including usage in decision making, review frequency, executive interest, and business alignment.

✅ Redundancy Detection

Automatically identifies metrics that overlap or duplicate information across departments, helping you consolidate and streamline your metrics dashboard.

✅ Smart Recommendations

Provides AI-generated recommendations on which metrics to keep, modify, or eliminate based on a comprehensive analysis of your entire metrics ecosystem.

How It Works

1. Upload your metrics data in CSV format
2. The AI analyzes each metric for impact and redundancy
3. Review visual dashboards highlighting key insights
4. Get specific recommendations on metrics to focus on
5. Export the analysis to Excel for further review
